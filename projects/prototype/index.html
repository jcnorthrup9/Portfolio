SP26, Prototype, 3GB Studio

Prototype

A generative pipeline for retail architecture and product design, built around a footwear brand's flagship storefront. Architectural iterations were produced through a custom Flux/ComfyUI pipeline, while the product line — a technical boot shown here in cutaway and colorway studies — was developed in parallel through a Gemini-based image pipeline. The two threads feed each other: the building's industrial material palette of raw wood, steel, and concrete is carried directly into the product's own construction and display.
